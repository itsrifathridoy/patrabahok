How delivery works

  • Each member gets their own copy. Replies go to the sender, not the group.
  • A member that is itself a group receives the mail too, so groups can be nested.
  • Posting rules are checked when a message arrives, and senders who aren't allowed get an immediate error.
  • People on this server must be signed in to their mail account to post to a restricted group.
